## Notes from the CEO

> "Q3 numbers aren't where we want them. New customer acquisition has slowed for three straight months. Our existing customers are expanding less than they were, and more of them are leaving. Sales is blaming marketing for the lead quality issue, marketing is blaming product for the conversion drop, product is blaming sales for discounting too aggressively. Nobody's owning it.
>
> We have a board meeting on Oct 28. I need a one-page dashboard I can hand to the board that doesn't hide anything but also doesn't panic them. Show me what changed, what's off, and what we're doing about it.
>
> If you can produce something the board can read in 60 seconds and walk away with the right impression, you're a hero. If they have to call me after the meeting for clarification, we're in trouble."

## What the board cares about (per the last board meeting prep doc)

- **Path to default alive.** Are we going to run out of money before we hit the next milestone? If yes, when do we need to raise?
- **What's working.** Are there any bright spots we should double down on?
- **What's broken.** What's the one thing we'd fix if we could only fix one thing?
- **Are we moving fast enough.** Velocity of customer acquisition, velocity of churn remediation.

## Internal context the VP wants the dashboard to reflect

- The new VP of Sales (started in August) is implementing a new ICP definition and outbound playbook. Early signal: outbound meeting rate is up 40%, but conversion to closed-won is still flat.
- Customer Success rolled out a new health scoring model in July. It flagged 23 accounts as "at risk." The CSM team saved 11 of them; the other 12 churned.
- Marketing ran an ABM pilot in September targeting 30 enterprise accounts. 4 opportunities created, 1 closed ($45K ARR). Below the $180K ARR target from the original pilot.
- Engineering shipped the v3 API in August. Adoption is slower than expected — only 23% of new customers are using it by week 30.

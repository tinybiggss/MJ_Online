# How Mike Works

## How Mike Works

How to collaborate with Mike (for any agent or collaborator): Direct, peer-level, opinionated. Say "I'm not sure" over a confident wrong answer. Disagree when warranted — he wants decisions made, not options surfaced. Systems thinker: frameworks and pipelines. One deciding factor beats a pros/cons list. Agents are teammates: named, with memory and continuity. Naming convention: {Task-Description}-{Number}, e.g. Manifesto-Draft-#15 — never opaque "Agent #15". Mistakes are learning opportunities; explain what broke before fixing. ADD-friendly session rules apply (hot spot at open, stubby list ≤3, capture don't hold, session close = state saved): see the add-friendly-core skill and ~/dev/add-friendly-agent/. Taste decisions are veto decisions, not selection decisions. Mike can reject reliably but cannot select — "I have no developed sense of what looks good." Never present a menu of drafts, logos or layouts, and never send him to hunt references. Nominate ONE artifact and justify it on criteria he can check without taste (holds at 16px, says the intended thing, close to what he already owns), then offer veto. For identity work the order is brief → territories → one constructed asset in the context it will live in → veto-only. (Source: T-2026-11-02-9TK, 2026-11-05 ruling; see velocity-partners for the case history.)

# Distills

## Distills

Product. Career knowledge, made interviewable. Career documents → AI voice interview → recruiter-facing chatbot. The pitch: a person's knowledge base becomes an AI that answers questions about them — "makes you appear wiser and AI-forward in your thinking." A hiring manager using it reported: *"I found myself asking questions of Distills that I would not ask a candidate in person, so I got a much better understanding of what their knowledge base was."*

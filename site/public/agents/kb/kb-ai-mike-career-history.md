# Mike Jones — Career History (public facts)

Mike Jones has 29 years in gaming, entertainment, and media technology. Through-line in his own words: define the product, then ship it. This page carries the public, citable career facts so an agent can reference them accurately. Engagement and offers live on the business pages, not here.

### Microsoft — Xbox and Xbox 360 launch teams (1999–2006)

Core team for both the Xbox and Xbox 360 platform launches. Led test teams that shipped 6 AAA launch titles across the two platforms, and set pre-launch quality standards for SDK tooling, usability frameworks, and instrumentation strategy that became foundational for Microsoft's gaming division. Holds a patent on VINCE — a video-game instrumentation method shipped in the Xbox SDK. It captured what players actually did for usability testing (first shipped in Voodoo Vince and Halo 2) and later became the kill-shot replay feature across the Halo franchise. The design judgment he carries from it: ask what the player did, not just whether they beat the level.

### Director-level builds: Kabam, Kinoo, Verizon

Kinoo — VP of Production & Product (2020–2022): first hire; built the PMO from zero and led production across hardware, firmware, app, UX, backend, marketing, and user testing. 120+ people, $12M+ budget over 2 years, 100% launch success rate. Shipped the Kinoo Wand — AR hardware with computer vision and ML, paired with a Unity iOS/Android app on an AWS backend. Result: 10 industry awards in 2021–2022 including CES Innovation 2022 and the Good Housekeeping Best Toy Award, plus an AARP Innovation Lab win. Kabam — Director-level PMO build: $4.5M program, 70+ staff. Verizon — Director-level PMO build: 250+ people across 5 departments.

# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary: consulting clients and executives** evaluating Mike Jones (via Velocity Partners) for AI-implementation work. They are decision-makers — founders, VPs, directors, operators — arriving with a problem ("AI isn't landing inside our org") and limited time. They want fast proof of credibility: real outcomes, real scope, evidence he has done this before and can be trusted with budget and teams.

**Secondary audiences** (must be served without diluting the primary):
- Recruiters / hiring managers assessing director/VP/principal fit.
- Resilient Tomorrow readers and Get Hired Now Academy (GHN) course audience — the human/vision story.
- Peers, founders, and potential collaborators evaluating technical depth and authenticity.

Context of use: skimming on desktop or mobile, often referred or arriving from LinkedIn/a proposal. The site has ~10 seconds to establish "this person is the real thing."

## Product Purpose

A personal platform and portfolio for Mike Jones — AI Implementation Expert & LLM Integration Specialist with 29 years building systems that ship. It presents an interconnected ecosystem of work (Velocity Partners consulting, GHN teaching, Resilient Tomorrow publishing, Distills product) under one through-line: *helping people and organizations operate at the intersection of AI, autonomy, and resilience.*

It replaces a Ghost-hosted site, will be self-hosted as a static site on Cloudflare Pages, and embeds Mike's own Distills career chatbot (dogfooding the product). Success = a client or exec leaves convinced enough to start a conversation, and the site itself demonstrates the AI fluency Mike sells.

## Brand Personality

**Confident · technical · grounded.** Voice is plain-spoken expert: authoritative without hype, specific instead of buzzwordy, generous and human without being soft. Mike has nothing to prove and everything to show — the tone is "here's the work" rather than "here's my pitch." Earned confidence from someone who has shipped at Microsoft and built his own AI stack, not a personal brand performing expertise.

## Anti-references

The site must NOT look like any of these:
- **Generic AI/SaaS template** — dark gradient hero, decorative glass cards, tiny uppercase eyebrow over every section, the big-number hero-metric stat row. The 2026 AI-generated look. (Note: avoid even when a "cosmic/aurora" direction is tempting.)
- **Corporate-consultant bland** — stock photography, blue-and-gray safety palette, McKinsey-speak, lifeless and risk-averse.
- **Crypto/web3 hype** — neon overload, aggressive gradients, gimmicky motion, hype language.
- **Cluttered personal blog** — busy, unfocused, widget soup, dated WordPress-template energy.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Lead with shipped work, real metrics, and named outcomes. Execs trust evidence, not adjectives. Every claim should be backed by something concrete.
2. **Practice what you preach.** The site is itself an artifact of Mike's craft and AI fluency — the Distills bot, the polish, the restraint all demonstrate the capability he sells.
3. **One body of work.** Consulting, teaching, publishing, and product must read as a coherent, mutually-reinforcing ecosystem — not a scattered list of side projects.
4. **Grounded authority.** Confidence carried by substance and clarity, never by visual aggression or hype. When in doubt, remove flourish and let the work and the words stand.
5. **Respect the visitor's time.** Ruthless hierarchy. An exec should grasp who Mike is and why he matters within seconds, then be able to go deeper on their terms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (≥3:1 large), full keyboard navigation, semantic markup, visible focus states. All motion (notably any animated background) must honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with a static fallback. Responsive and legible from 320px up.
